    How to play

    Touch

    • Drag sideways to move
    • Tap the right side to rotate ↻, left side ↺
    • Two-finger tap rotates 180° (enable in Settings)
    • Drag down to soft drop
    • Flick down to hard drop
    • Flick up to hold

    Keyboard

    • ← → move, ↓ soft drop
    • ↑ / X rotate ↻, Z rotate ↺, A 180°
    • Space hard drop, C / Shift hold
    • Esc / P pause

    Modes

    • Marathon: endless, faster every 10 lines
    • Sprint: clear 40 lines as fast as you can
    • Ultra: best score in 2 minutes
    • Zen: no speed-up and no game over

    Install it: on iPhone/iPad tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline.
